Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version

  • AIMONX has no accounts and no AIMONX servers. We never see your data.
  • Your logbook is encrypted on your iPhone. By default, an encrypted copy is saved to your own iCloud, so nothing gets lost when you switch phones. You can turn it off.
  • If you put a range on the map, its location stays in your logbook, encrypted. It never reaches us.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your logbook never reaches us, so there's nothing to sell or share.
  • This website uses no cookies and no trackers.

Who we are

AIMONX is made by Piermichele Fassio, an independent developer based in Italy ("we", "us"). Under EU data protection law (the GDPR), we're the data controller for the small amount of personal data covered here.

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@aimonx.app.

The AIMONX app

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you put in AIMONX stays in AIMONX: your firearms, serial numbers if you choose to add them, round counts, range sessions, matches, ammo, handloads, maintenance, renewals, costs, notes, photos, the PDFs you attach to your sessions, and your ranges and gun shops, with their spot on the map if you add one.

It's stored in an encrypted database on your iPhone, and it only unlocks when you verify it's you. There's no AIMONX server holding a copy, so there's nothing for us to read.

Your encrypted copy in iCloud

By default, AIMONX saves an encrypted copy of your logbook to your own iCloud account, so a new or broken phone doesn't mean starting over.

  • The copy is encrypted on your iPhone, with the same key as your logbook, before it goes anywhere.
  • It sits in your personal iCloud storage. We can't access it.
  • You can turn it off anytime in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Saved to iCloud > See All > AIMONX. Your logbook then stays only on your iPhone.

iCloud is run by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What we don't collect

No account, no email address, no name, no contacts, no usage analytics, no advertising IDs. We don't collect your location either: if you put a place on the map, it stays in your logbook. AIMONX doesn't include any third-party code that talks to the internet: no analytics, no crash reporters, no ad networks.

Location

AIMONX uses location for one thing only: putting your ranges and gun shops on the map. It's optional, and you can do it three ways:

  • Tap Use Current Location. The first time, your iPhone asks for permission. AIMONX gets your location only when you tap it, while you're using the app, and never in the background.
  • Tap Choose on Map and drop the pin yourself.
  • Let AIMONX find the spot from the address you entered. To do that, it sends the address to Apple's map service, only when you tap Choose on Map.

The location is saved with the place in your encrypted logbook, and in your encrypted iCloud copy and archives. It's never sent to us. Sessions don't get a location of their own: they show the map of their range.

Maps and directions are provided by Apple (MapKit and Apple Maps) and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Photos

You can add photos of your firearms, sessions, and ammo from Photos or Files. AIMONX never uses your camera. Before a photo is saved, AIMONX strips out its location and other metadata, then encrypts it like the rest of your logbook. Your photos are included in your encrypted iCloud copy.

You can also attach up to 3 PDFs to a session, like match results or stage times. Each one is encrypted with its own key, like your photos, and included in your encrypted iCloud copy and your archives. PDFs are saved exactly as they are. Unlike photos, AIMONX doesn't strip anything out of them, so details stored inside the file, like its author or creation date, stay there.

PDF reports and archives

PDF reports and encrypted archives are created on your iPhone. They go wherever you choose to send them, and nowhere else. Once you share a file, it's up to the app or service you sent it to.

Purchases

AIMONX Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details.

Crash reports and statistics from Apple

If you've turned on Share With App Developers on your iPhone, Apple may share with us statistics and diagnostic information about the app, aggregated or in a form that doesn't identify you. That's Apple's feature, not ours, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Your data is yours. Tap Reset App in the app's settings to delete your logbook from your iPhone. AIMONX also deletes your encrypted iCloud copy (you'll need a connection for that part).

Deleting the app removes the logbook from your iPhone, but your encrypted iCloud copy stays, so you can pick up where you left off if you reinstall. That's what the copy is for.

This website

aimonx.app is a static website. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no forms, and it loads nothing from other servers: even the fonts come from aimonx.app.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every site hosted there, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub is based in the U.S. and says it's certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

We don't track you, so "Do Not Track" signals don't change anything here.

Emailing us

If you email support@aimonx.app, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, we keep it only for as long as we need it to help you, and we never share it or add you to a mailing list. Our email is handled by Apple's iCloud Mail.

Legal bases (for our EU visitors and users)

  • Answering your emails: our legitimate interest in helping you.
  • Hosting logs kept by GitHub: a legitimate interest in keeping the site secure.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to get a copy of it. In practice, the only personal data we can ever hold is what you send us by email. Your logbook lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, where you're in control.

If you're in the EU, you can also file a complaint with your data protection authority. In Italy, that's the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

US privacy laws

We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't collect it for advertising or profiling.

Children

AIMONX isn't intended for children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
