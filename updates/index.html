What's new in AIMONX

Updated November 24, 2026

Every update to the app, newest first.

Enjoying the updates? Leave a review on the App Store — it helps other shooters find AIMONX.

Version 1.2.0 — November 22, 2026

  • More for free: up to 3 active firearms, 1 active reloaded load, and 3 maintenance rules per firearm
  • Advanced Protection and encrypted archives are now free, too
  • Sessions: up to 7 photos and up to 3 PDFs, and the first photo can be the session's icon
  • Dry fire has its own stats now: sessions, reps, and duration. Dry fire sessions log a duration instead of a start time
  • Copy Last Session now copies your last session of the type you pick
  • Bigger text: AIMONX follows the text size you set on your iPhone, all the way up to the largest standard size
  • The lock screen now covers any open form or settings page, too
  • You're only asked to choose between your iPhone and iCloud copies when they're actually different
  • Plus lots of fixes and polish

Version 1.1.0 — November 2, 2026

  • Photos for your firearms, sessions, and ammo. Make the first one the icon
  • Time-based maintenance: by days or months, by round count, or both. Early alerts are now free, too
  • Reload costs from what you paid (Pro): enter the price of your bullets, primers, brass, and powder, and AIMONX works out the cost per round
  • Muzzle velocity, Power Factor, and energy for every load, plus a Power Factor Calculator on the Home screen
  • Your ranges on the map, with directions in Apple Maps
  • Sessions: distance, weather, wind, temperature, and Copy Last Session
  • Firearms: capacity and technical specs (action, trigger, finish, barrel)
  • Ammo: custom icons, out-of-stock ammo stays visible, and 12-month stats
  • Your encrypted iCloud copy updates more often, and there's a new Back Up Now button
  • Now in French and Spanish
  • Try AIMONX Pro free for 1 month (new subscribers)

Version 1.0.1 — October 14, 2026

  • New Back Up Now button on the AIMONX Backup page, so you can back up to iCloud whenever you want.
  • Your iCloud backup now also catches up when you open the app, not only when you leave it.
  • Fixed a crash when deleting a firearm.
  • Large logs now use much less memory while backing up.

Version 1.0.0 — October 8, 2026

The first release. Log your firearms, range sessions and matches. Track rounds, ammo, maintenance, renewals and costs. Create PDF reports of your firearms. Your logbook is encrypted on your iPhone, with an encrypted copy in your own iCloud.

AIMONX — the technical logbook app for sport shooters.
