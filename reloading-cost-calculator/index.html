How the math works

Each component costs what you paid, divided by how many you got. Powder is the one that takes a step more: its price, divided by the container weight in grains, times your charge. A pound is 7,000 grains, so an 8 lb jug at $280 works out to half a cent a grain, and a 4.2-grain charge costs about two cents.

Keep it, batch after batch

This page does the math once. AIMONX keeps your recipes on your iPhone, gives every batch you load its own cost, and adds what you shoot to each range session, so you always know what a day at the range really cost you.

The printable load card is a website tool. In the app, your recipes live on your iPhone, along with every batch you load.

Free and Pro

The calculator on this page is free for everyone. In the app, your first handload is free, with its recipe, component costs and batches; Pro lets you keep as many as you want. The Power Factor calculator is free in both.
