Everything AIMONX keeps track of

One app for your firearms, your rounds, your ammo and your maintenance. Pick a feature to see how it works.

  • Round count

    Every firearm, every round.

    Every firearm, with its accessories and details, in one place.
    Rounds add up on their own from your range sessions — no mental math, no notebook.

    See how round count works

  • Range log

    Range sessions and matches.

    Record where you shot, what you shot and how it went — including malfunctions.
    Pin your ranges on the map. Matches also record your placement, points and division.

    See how the range log works

  • Ammo and costs

    Ammo you can count on.

    Factory ammo with lot numbers and inventory. Handloads with recipes, component costs and batches.
    Muzzle velocity, Power Factor and energy for every load.

    See how ammo tracking works

  • Handloads

    Your handloads, down to the cent

    If you reload, you already know your recipe. AIMONX keeps it, along with what it costs you per round, the batches you've loaded and how they perform. Then every range session uses them up and adds to your round count, like any other ammo.

    Handloads

  • Power Factor calculator

    Power Factor calculator, right on your iPhone

    Making major or minor? Enter the bullet weight and the muzzle velocity, and AIMONX shows the Power Factor and the muzzle energy as you type. It's free, and it's one tap from the Home screen.

    Power Factor calculator

  • Maintenance

    Maintenance your way.

    Create your own rules for each firearm — cleaning, lubrication, spring changes, anything — by round count, by time, or both, whichever comes first. Choose how early you want the heads-up, and AIMONX tells you inside the app when something is due.

    See how maintenance works

  • Custom lists

    Your lists, your way.

    Can't find your caliber or firearm type? Add it. Swipe away what you never use. Every list you pick from is yours to shape.

    See how custom lists work

  • Renewals you won't miss.

    Licenses and permits, club and range memberships, insurance — with a reminder as many days ahead as you like.

  • What your shooting really costs.

    Cost per session, per round and per firearm, and how practice compares to competition.

  • Stats that show your progress.

    Rounds over time and by firearm, your most-used ammo, each firearm's reliability and the ranges you visit most. See how your shooting is really going.

  • Firearm records

    Firearm reports in PDF.

    Specs, accessories and up to 5 photos for each firearm. A record sheet for each one and a full inventory — generated on your iPhone, optionally password-protected, shared only when you choose.

    See firearm records

  • Your data

    No account. No AIMONX servers. We never see your data.

    Your logbook lives encrypted on your iPhone. An encrypted copy is kept in your own iCloud — on by default, and you can turn it off — so a new phone doesn't mean starting over. Serial numbers are optional.

    See how AIMONX keeps your data private
