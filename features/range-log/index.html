A range log that fills itself in

Log a session in a few taps, and your round count, ammo and maintenance all update on their own. Next time, start from the last one.

What goes into a range session in AIMONX?

The firearm, date, range and distance, the ammo and rounds you shot, costs, malfunctions, weather, a star rating and notes, plus up to 7 photos and 3 PDFs. Save it, and your round count, ammo and maintenance update on their own. Next time, Copy Last Session fills in the same firearm, range, distance and ammo for that type of session.

What goes in a session

The firearm, the date, the type of session, the range and the distance. The ammo you shot, one line per load, with the rounds for each. Lane fees and other costs, any malfunctions, the weather, a star rating and notes. Plus up to 7 photos, like your targets, and up to 3 PDFs, like match results or stage times.

Copy the last session

Shoot the same setup every week? Pick the session type and tap Copy Last Session: the firearm, range, distance and ammo from your last session of that type are already filled in. Today's date, empty round counts, and you're done.

Your ranges, on the map

Save your ranges and gun shops with their address and contact info. Then drop a pin: use your current location, tap the spot on the map, or let AIMONX suggest it from the address. Every session at that range shows the map, and Directions opens Apple Maps to get you there.

Maps and address search come from Apple and only run when you tap. Your pins stay in your encrypted logbook, never sent to us.

Dry fire counts as practice, not rounds

Log dry fire as its own session with your reps and how long you practiced. It never touches your round count, your ammo or your maintenance, and your stats count it on its own line.

Matches, too

With Pro, log a match with its discipline, division and level, plus your place, points, percentage and time. AIMONX tracks your best results and how you're trending.

Free and Pro

Sessions are unlimited in Free, and so are ranges, maps, photos and PDFs. Matches are part of Pro.

Private by design

Your sessions and your ranges stay encrypted on your iPhone. No account. No AIMONX servers. We never see your data.
